Components / Tab

Tab

Organize and allow navigation between groups of content that are related and at the same level of hierarchy.

Preview (Line Variant)

Account Settings

Manage your account settings and preferences here. Notice how the active tab has a solid brand underline.

Security

Update your password and robust security questions to protect your account from unauthorized access.

Alerts

Configure how and when you want to be notified about important events related to your services.

This content should not be visible normally because the tab is disabled.

Preview (Pill Variant)

Pill tabs are useful for inner-page navigation where a line-style tab might conflict with a higher-level navigation element.

Analytics view. The active state resembles a raised card on a slightly darker background.

Reports and exported data view.

Usage

HTML 
<!-- Wrapper -->
<div class="tabs">
    <!-- Tab List -->
    <div class="tab-list" role="tablist">
        <button class="tab-trigger active" data-target="tab1">Tab 1</button>
        <button class="tab-trigger" data-target="tab2">Tab 2</button>
    </div>
    
    <!-- Panels -->
    <div class="tab-panel active" id="tab1">Content 1</div>
    <div class="tab-panel" id="tab2">Content 2</div>
</div>
